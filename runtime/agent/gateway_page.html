How a job is checked

A provider's GPU computes each request as a job on the chain, and the chain pays it from your jobs balance. An answer's trust grows as the job moves on:

  1. committed: the results match what the provider committed on chain (about a minute).
  2. verified: a committee of staked verifiers that hold the model recomputed sampled stages and agreed.
  3. final: the challenge window passed with no successful challenge; the provider is paid.

If a provider loses a dispute, the job is posted again for free. Inputs are public on the network's data service: only send text that may be published.
