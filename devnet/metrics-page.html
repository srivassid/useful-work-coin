Definitions and what is left out
Useful share
Settled stages times the linear-layer MACs of one stage, over the MACs that difficulty implies were hashed (2^256 / target per block).
Verification overhead
Seconds a verifier spent recomputing its sampled stages, over the seconds the provider spent on the whole job. Verifiers here recompute each sampled stage from the tokens, including every earlier layer group.
Client price vs renting
The job's fee over the provider's GPU seconds times what that GPU would earn noise mining in the same time. Below 1 means the provider earned less than mining would have paid.
False slash
A failed job whose provider had computed every stage honestly.
Live λ
Only the provider on .245 mines while serving. It runs the reference runtime, whose emulated float ops dominate its time; the measured λ table shows the fast path from step 1.
Left out
Jackpots: decoy outcomes count on chain once the council reveals its forced-error record, after the last decoy ends.
